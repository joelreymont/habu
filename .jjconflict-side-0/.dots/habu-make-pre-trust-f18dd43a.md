The engine decides whether a `defer NAME ( E )` can register its effect row and defer row with the checker by asking whether a word named `trust` is resolvable (C-PRETRUST-READY?, bootstrap/cg/forth.fs and src/habu/habu2.f). That is an existence proxy for the real question, which is whether THIS load's checker is live. A stale checker satisfies the proxy: the recovery engine reads the boot prefix from disk at startup and then interprets its baked program, which loads the prefix again, so while src/core/checker.f is being re-read the previous load's `trust` and `checker-defer` are still resolvable. The pre-trust defers then publish into the checker that is being replaced, the pending table stays empty, DRAIN-PRETRUST replays nothing, and the first checked `is` on such a defer fails to certify. Dot habu-fix-stage0-pre-88a4297e removed the situation by emitting the boot-hide prologue for every consumer of tools/bootstrap.sh emit_src, which is correct and is what unblocked recovery, but the proxy itself is still there and will mis-answer again for any other route that leaves a stale checker resolvable. Task: replace the proxy with a handshake. The checker already tells the engine when its registration surface becomes usable, with the bare DRAIN-PRETRUST token right after `: TRUST` (src/core/checker.f:8725). Add the matching signal at the other end: a primitive the checker calls near the top of its own source, before the pre-trust defers at src/core/checker.f:460, that marks the engine's notion of "checker live" as false for the load now beginning. C-PRETRUST-READY? then means "this load's checker is live", so a defer declared during a checker re-load is captured and replayed by that load's drain no matter what an older dictionary still holds. Do it in both engines with the same bytes (bootstrap/cg/forth.fs and src/habu/habu2.f) and keep the SEAL-CAPTURE backstop (BSEALCAP, exit 73) intact. Forbidden: naming any defer, naming `trust` in a colon-definition special case, relaxing what `is` accepts, and any value heuristic such as comparing xt addresses or dictionary indices. Acceptance: a minimal checked fixture in which a checker source is loaded twice in one process registers its pre-trust defers into the second load's checker; test/pre-trust-defer.f stays green; the recovery run stays green with the boot-hide prologue AND with it removed from the seed, since the handshake makes the prologue no longer load-bearing for this property. Related: habu-reprove-the-undrained-16d55d36 (native undrained backstop), habu-fix-stage0-pre-88a4297e (the precondition fix).
