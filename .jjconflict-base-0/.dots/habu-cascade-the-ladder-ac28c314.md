Problem: CODEGEN-CORPUS4:LADDER (tools/codegen-compare-corpus4.f:294-303) is 168 chain bytes against clang's 104, and the 64-byte gap is NOT the if-conversion being too narrow in shape - it is register pressure, measured. The ladder's TAIL already converts: migrating the row's exact source alone through the production entry (src/compiler/native/migrate.f:858 NMIGRATE:DEFINE, 1 1 8) and printing every candidate region at src/compiler/native/select.f:2919 R-TRY gives four tries - head 9 with 15 members carrying 9 values, head 12 with 12 members carrying 8, head 15 with 9 members carrying 6, and head 18 with 6 members carrying 4, which is the only one COMMITTED (width 1). The first three are refused by R-CARRY-OK? (select.f:2903) against SEL-CARRY-MAX (select.f:342). Every one of those regions reports LOCAL 0: nothing in the ladder is an arm's own local, so the carried-value widening of habu-widen-if-conversion-d7ec28d0 cannot reach this row at all - the values are guard literals read by the comparison each branch fuses with, and answer constants handed to the join, both CARRIED by R-CARRIED? (select.f:2531). Blocker two is why the bound may not simply be raised: with SEL-CARRY-MAX at 6, 7 or 8 the head-15 region is admitted and the SAME migration of the SAME source throws -8508, E-A64RA-SPILL, from the register allocator (src/compiler/native/regalloc.f:1857, the position where no class holding a register can be taken) - the whole routine stops compiling. Measured at bounds 4,5 (164 record bytes, branches kept, answers 0 1 2 8) and 6,7,8 (-8508). So the ladder needs FEWER held values, not a larger bound: the guard literals want a compare-immediate form so no register holds them, or the answer constants want rematerialising at the select instead of being held from their arm to the join, or the cascade wants to be built as ccmp/csel with each answer materialised beside its own select. Acceptance: LADDER's chain bytes fall toward clang's 104 with its answers unchanged, every corpus row still compiles, tools/codegen-compare.f reports no regression and the native select/regalloc/emit/chain suites stay green; whichever direction is taken, the region that is admitted must allocate - a bound raised without cutting the held set is proven here to end in E-A64RA-SPILL.
