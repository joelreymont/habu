Problem (kevin, Tender review): whole-answer mode grows the response body in an open_memstream (src/lib/net/curl.f STREAM-OPEN/STREAM-TAKE for PERFORM, the R.BODY record for START/AWAIT) with no receive bound, so a body grows without limit and each realloc leaves copies of secrets (OAuth token answers, vault answers) in freed heap; the finished body and the published response headers are freed without a wipe. Decision (carl, Joel deferred to the best long-term choice, 2026-10-28): the body is received straight into the caller's span and bounded by it. Acceptance: PERFORM and START/AWAIT write the body into the caller's destination span through a fixed-buffer unbuffered stream (no heap copy of the body anywhere in curl.f); a body that does not fit stops the transfer and answers truncated, whose len is now the bytes the span holds (ftell of the span stream; glibc can stop one byte short of capacity), and curl.f:10's contract says so; a known Content-Length past capacity is refused before receiving (CURLOPT_MAXFILESIZE_LARGE) and answers truncated with len 0; a failed or cancelled transfer may leave part of the body in the span; a halted owner's abandon returns only after the loop has removed its easy handle, so no write reaches a span whose owner has left; capacity 0 is defined; every header buffer and any remaining response buffer is wiped before free; the header comment names what stays outside the guarantee (libcurl's own receive and TLS buffers). No variant shape change. Files: src/lib/net/curl.f, src/lib/net/curl-test.f, src/lib/net/http-test.f if it reads truncated. Verify: curl-test, http-test, browser host-test; local-server cases for body < = > capacity, chunked overflow, Content-Length overflow, capacity 0, on both PERFORM and START/AWAIT. Depends: none.
