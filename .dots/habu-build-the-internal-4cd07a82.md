Problem: src/habu/prof.f is a 1 ms SIGALRM sampler that attributes the interrupted pc to a word by scanning every 48-byte dictionary record in the handler (habu-idx-profiler-pc-45b4c841 measured 21000 to 43000 instructions per tick), records exclusive counts only, prints unqualified names, cannot stop without exiting, and knows nothing about callers. It cannot answer the questions we ask of it: what the 138 s self-build spends its time on and who calls the hot word, why a stripped Loom or Maki program is slow, what a startup does for 25 ms. Tonight the build was profiled with perf plus tools/imgdump.f --pc because our own profiler could not; Tender asked on 2026-09-13 for shared primitives attributed to their callers. Needs, all of them: (1) pc to word attribution in O(log n) through a pc-sorted live-range index built at prof-on, never a dictionary walk in the handler, correct for tier-0, tier-1 and JIT words and for anonymous quotations; (2) caller attribution: inclusive counts and call chains from the sampled machine stack; emitted words carry no frame pointer (prologue is sub sp,sp,#16 / str x30,[sp]), so either the handler walks the machine stack conservatively (bounded scan for return addresses inside the code range, the standard frame-pointer-less technique) or the compiler adds an x29 chain, decided by measurement of user-code cost (Joel will not accept per-word bloat for the default build; a profiling build flag is acceptable if the chain is needed); (3) package-qualified names in every row; (4) prof-on / prof-off / prof-report / prof-reset usable from a program and from the command line, sampling one phase without exiting, counters readable after prof-off; (5) works while profiling the engine itself during tools/native-build.f and inside stripped images (the profiler band lives in DATA); (6) bounded handler cost (target under 2 us per tick), configurable rate, no allocation in the handler, foreign-code ticks still counted as (foreign); (7) reports: exclusive and inclusive counts with percent, top callers per word, a callee tree, text and JSON, sorted, with a documented format in docs/debugging.md; (8) a tools/perf-map.f that maps perf script addresses to qualified words so external perf with call chains stays usable as a cross-check. Acceptance: a gate fixture with a synthetic workload of known proportions (three words at 60/30/10 percent plus a shared primitive called from two of them) whose exclusive, inclusive and caller rows match within tolerance; the native build profiled end to end with the top ten rows and their callers explaining at least 80 percent of samples; measured overhead under 5 percent at 1 kHz; docs/debugging.md rewritten for the new surface. Files: src/habu/prof.f, src/habu/habu2.f (prologue only if the x29 chain is chosen), tools/perf-map.f, docs/debugging.md, test/gate-debug-lib.f, a new profiler fixture. Verify: the fixture through bin/hb, then hb --load tools/native-build.f under prof-on with the report attached to the dot. Depends: habu-idx-profiler-pc-45b4c841 and habu-finish-the-profiler-4cdd3b18 are the first two slices of this dot and close into it. Ownership: hazel line.
