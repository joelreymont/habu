Problem: src/compiler/native/regalloc.f (A64RA, 2335 lines) and regalloc-verify.f (A64RAV, 2442 lines) are dialect-neutral except ~30 lines of ARM64 SPELLINGS: BIND-DIALECT (regalloc.f ~2117-2131, regalloc-verify.f ~2330-2340) interns A64IR:GPR-TYPE/FPR-TYPE/MEM-TYPE, the keys SLOT/FRAME/DSLOT/DBYTES/DBACK/DWB/ENTRY/TRAP-ENTRY/ADDR/SHIFT and the MOV and MOVZ opcodes by name; DIALECT-CK (~2082, ~2245) compares A64IR:NAME/MAJOR/MINOR; regalloc.f TARGET-CK (~2066) refuses any context whose arch is not AARCH64 (the one line that refuses an X64IR module today); ADDRESS-HALF (~479) and three A64IR:HALVES readers (~1350, ~1761, ~1823) know that an address literal is a four-lane movz/movk run; regalloc-verify.f reads A64IR:SLOT-WIDTH at ten sites and A64FRAME:LINK-SLOT/LINK-KEPT?/SPILL-BASE at three (which already answer 0 for a link-absent contract). X64IR has every identity except x64.dwb (no write-back addressing, by design) and has MOVI where A64IR has MOVZ; an address literal is one instruction (carrier lanes = 1, x64.movi carries x64.addr but no x64.shift). Acceptance: A64RA:BIND-DIALECT and A64RAV:BIND-DIALECT take the dialect's identities as an argument - a small vocabulary value (dialect name/major/minor, the three types, the keys with DWB optional, the copy opcode, the remat opcode, the address-carrier lane count, the slot width) built by a64ir.f for ARM64 and by x64ir.f for x86-64 - instead of naming A64IR inline; TARGET-CK becomes the registry check emit.f already uses (~1478 A64IR:CONTRACT@ CTARGET:EMITS? or the vocabulary's own arch); ADDRESS-HALF answers through the vocabulary (lane count 1 -> an address literal has no lane arithmetic); the slot width and link facts come from the vocabulary / NMACH; arm64 byte-identical across the generation chain and test/compiler/native-regalloc.f unchanged in what it asserts; PLUS the first x86-64 allocation: a test (test/compiler/x64-regalloc.f or a section of x64-select.f) that selects a leaf routine with X64SEL under an X64ABI:LEAF contract, binds A64RA with the x64 vocabulary and X64M:MACHINE, runs A64RA:ALLOCATE and A64RAV:ACCEPT, asserts ACCEPTED? and pins REG@/CLAIM@ for a two-value leaf that fits the nine registers (no spill pass needed: NEEDS-LOWERING? is false on PLAN-N 0=). Files: src/compiler/native/regalloc.f, regalloc-verify.f, a64ir.f, x64ir.f, src/arch/arm64/passes.f (the binding call), tests. Verify: native-regalloc, native-emit, native-chain, x64-select and the new suite; three generations with cmp; test/run.f. Depends: the two-address copy dot. Ownership: hazel. Claim: unassigned.
