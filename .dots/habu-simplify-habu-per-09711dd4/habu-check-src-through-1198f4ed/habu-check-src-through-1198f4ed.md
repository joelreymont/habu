Approach (Joel 2026-11-17, "Check by loading"): docs/architecture.md "Tools". `hb --check FILE... [-- args]` is the engine's multi-error load (checker.f's) with RUN-FOUND (src/habu/interpreter.f) as a state machine over the known stack depth: a found word runs when it has code, its `acts` fact is clear and no input it takes is a placeholder; otherwise the checker applies its type and its outputs are placeholders (a `parses:` row skips operands; an unbounded parser or a defer stops the check with one W-CHECK-DEFERRED; a name a renderer may make is W-CHECK-DEFERRED at use). Keywords, literals and loaders run; a rejected definition has no code; exit steps do not run. Verdict: verified, deferred or rejected. `acts` is a REACH class on primitive rows (open-for-write, unlink, rename, mkdir, rmdir, chmod, symlink, kill, setpgid, fork, execve, spawn-*, ffi-call*, dlopen-entry, ioctl, task-entry; not write or read) and a fifth inherited fact, facts tag `acts`; a stored or passed quotation is taken to act, a quotation literal or tick carries its word's facts, and a call charges a word with the facts of the quotations it takes. No guard in any primitive. Facts are rows their owners record, no hooks: definitions (record, file, name bytes), uses, files and loads (the loader's event log), open texts, the cursor's visible words; flags --facts N, --texts, --at N, --of TAIL; bin/hb.map gains the name's byte start and end; SOURCE-UNIT:USE and SOURCE-INPUT:USE become private. tools/check.f keeps --json-errors, --source-list, --stdin-path and --deadline-ms and loses --verify-only, --all-errors and its run stage. Order: engine check mode; check.f onto it; lsp-check, references and source-index; lsp-workspace; tests off VERIFY:*, then one commit deletes package VERIFY (verify-source.f), CHECK-ALL-ERRORS:*, DISCOVER:* (source-discovery.f), LSP-CHECK:VERIFY-BYTES*, PREVERIFY-* and INDEX-BYTES, the VERIFY-* owner slots, the DECL-VERIFY-* constants, the verifier's CHECKER-OVERLAY rows and replay registrars.
