Measured from CODEGEN-CORPUS2:T-RES-WALK-N against the engine's own T-RES-WALK. Three of the ten extra instructions are pointer moves whose net effect over the routine is nothing. The chain fixes the body's data-stack pointer at base = entry - 8*in and gets there with a DTAKE 'sub x19,#8' (offset 8), pushes it back up over the call's arguments with DBYTES 'add x19,#8' (offset 20), pulls it down over the call's results with DBACK 'sub x19,#16' (offset 28), and pushes it up again over its own results with DPUBLISH 'add x19,#8' (offset 64). The engine emits ONE pointer move in the whole word - 'sub x19,x19,#8' to pop the flag - because it never repositions: the pointer stays where its caller left it and every slot is addressed from there. For this routine the required positions are: the pointer at the branch-with-link must be entry (one argument sits at entry-8), and at the return it must be entry (one result sits at entry-8). Both are where the caller left it, so DTAKE, DBYTES and DPUBLISH are all zero and DBACK is 8 rather than 16 - three instructions gone and the remaining one identical to the engine's. What to build: a placement that chooses the pointer position at each program point as the position that needs the fewest adjustments, subject to the two constraints that really exist - a call's branch needs the pointer at the callee's argument base, and the return needs it at base+8*out - and then rewrites each DSLOT offset against the position that actually holds. The emitter must write no instruction for a zero adjustment, and the sign has to be handled because an adjustment can now go either way. THE ONE REAL OBSTACLE: a chosen position can put a remaining access at a NEGATIVE offset, and a64.aldr/a64.astr are the unsigned-offset forms. Either keep a position that leaves every remaining access non-negative (which is what T-RES-WALK-N gets for free once habu-keep-a-pass-8025401f has removed its other accesses) or add the LDUR/STUR signed-offset forms to the dialect. Say which, and prove it on the corpus. Owner: A64SEL and A64EMIT, with the re-derivation in src/compiler/native/regalloc-verify.f, whose VCALL-SITE today insists DBYTES and DBACK equal the lengths of the store and load runs beside them - a rule that has to become a statement about where the pointer ends up rather than about how many operations stand next to it. Depends on habu-keep-a-pass-8025401f for the case that motivates it. Acceptance: T-RES-WALK-N falls a further 12 bytes to 44, no row of any corpus regresses, tools/codegen-compare.f 0 findings.
