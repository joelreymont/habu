Maki R2-SW makes eleven SolidWorks files parts of SOLIDWORKS (Joel 2026-10-31, nested packages), and seven declare STRUCTUREs (all five colliding type names are STRUCTUREs), so R2-SW waits on this. Cause confirmed (jerry, hunt on the installed hb, 2026-11-10; it matches the design /Users/joel/Work/maki/.jj-ws.noindex/tim/.out/r2/design-structure-in-part.md, whose public items wait for Joel): src/core/sumtype.f:1352 TDGEN-FAM-REF writes TFAM-PKG$, a colon and the family's name, and a part's package is the path PA:PB, so the generated maker's signature says `PA:PB:point`; src/core/type-family.f:5263 TFQ-SPLIT? answers false at a second colon and the bare lookup finds no such family. `PB:point` resolves from PB, from PA and from a sibling part (CHECKER-RESOLVE:HEAD-ROW, checker.f:13565), so writing the path's last segment (LAST-SEGMENT$, type-family.f:2361) makes b.f and g.f load with their words in PB's private wordlist. The constructor package has the same fault: TF-CTOR-PKG$ (type-family.f:1239) gives `PA:PB-BOX`, which no reader resolves and `package` refuses, so a public family's words cannot be reached from PA or a sibling until that package is a part of PA (the design's choice); mapping the colon to `-` would make them reachable outside PA, and the last segment alone collides with a top-level PB. What declares inside a part today: a private SUMTYPE, ENUM and NEWTYPE and a LAYOUT-BUFFER load (they generate no words); a private PRODUCT is refused as the STRUCTURE is; a public STRUCTURE and a public ENUM of several variants are refused 'name is reserved or already taken' (rc 70), a public one-variant ENUM dies 'xref: generated declaration visibility mismatch' (rc 76), and a public SUMTYPE ends 'hb: uncaught throw code 7110' (rc 67) with no other line. Each of those four is an acceptance case here: a declaration in a part works or is refused by name.
