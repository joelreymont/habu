Problem: Habu has no WebAssembly target (no wasm row in src/compiler/target.f; C6 covers arm64, x86_64 and Cortex-M only). Maki's browser viewer is written in Habu and compiled to wasm32 (maki AGENTS.md, docs/habu-needs.md). Design: docs/wasm-backend.md (proposed 2026-09-29, audited against master aed8416b): memory32 with 64-bit cells; reuse the checker, recorded tape, elaborator and frozen HIR; a dedicated Wasm IR and binary emitted in checked Habu; no LLVM, MLIR, Emscripten or Binaryen in the production compiler; first product an AOT-compiled browser library behind a small host adapter; long-term checked interactive Habu and a self-hosting fixed point in the browser. Re-audit section 2 of the design against master before W0, since its baseline predates this dot. Acceptance: the waves in docs/wasm-backend.md section 15, each a child dot when started: W0 portability contracts and negative tests; W1 honest scalar backend (Habu-generated bytes validate and execute, arithmetic parity including MIN-N -1 / and zero division); W2 control flow and checked data; W3 useful browser library (no native Habu process; Chromium, Firefox, WebKit); W4 dynamic runtime correctness; W5 browser compilation and evaluation; W6 self-hosting and qualification (B1/B2 identity); W7 optimization and additional embeddings. Files: design section 14 (src/arch/wasm/, src/compiler/ target vocabulary, host/browser/, test/wasm/). Overlaps: src/compiler/native/ (hir, elaborate, frozen, publish) and target.f touch alder's and Heron's lanes; send each wave's bounded design and file ownership to alder before implementation. Verify: per wave, section 15 acceptance and section 16 test matrix.
