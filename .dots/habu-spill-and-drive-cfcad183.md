Problem: after the allocator binds to a dialect vocabulary, an X64IR module that needs spills, remats or moves has no rewrite pass: src/compiler/native/spill.f (A64SPILL, 1155 lines) rebuilds the module in ARM64 forms - it binds the whole A64IR opcode table and all sixteen keys (four X64IR lacks: dwb, off, mask, throw-entry), names LINKSAVE/LINKLOAD in its frame-shape arithmetic, and emits RESERVE/RELEASE/STORE/LOAD/MOV/MOVZ - and there is no src/arch/x86-64/passes.f. prune.f (A64PRUNE) is a no-op on the whole corpus (its header says so; A64PASS:PRUNE short-circuits on REWRITES = 0). Acceptance: (1) X64IR gains BIND-OPCODES! (a64ir.f ~830 is the shape); (2) an X64SPILL twin of spill.f over X64IR: the ~700 lines of frozen-module cursor, value map, block staging and plan cursor reused as they are (factor them out if the two files would otherwise duplicate them - one authority), the ~200 lines of dialect-specific parts written for x86-64 (six opcode constants without LINKSAVE/LINKLOAD, the twelve-key binding, COPY-ATTRS over those keys, EMIT-RESERVE/RELEASE/STORE/LOAD/MOVE and EMIT-REMAT as x64.movi, the frame shape with no link slot); (3) src/arch/x86-64/passes.f (X64PASS) as the near-copy of src/arch/arm64/passes.f: DECLARE/ROUTINE over X64ABI, SELECT over X64SEL, PRUNE installed as NBACK:NO-REWRITE, LOWERED = X64SPILL:REWRITE, KEEP-FRAME/NEEDS-LOWERING?/FIXPOINT, ARCH = CTARGET-ARCH:X86-64, INSTALL filling the NBACK rows - EMIT left as the refusal the registry gives a passless arch until emit-x64.f lands; (4) a test that drives a routine with more live values than nine registers through select -> allocate -> spill -> allocate -> verify and pins the spill slots and the frame. Files: src/compiler/native/x64ir.f, a new src/compiler/native/spill-x64.f (or the shared core + two thin halves), src/arch/x86-64/passes.f, src/arch/x86-64/backend.f, tests. Verify: native-chain, native-regalloc, the new suite; three generations with cmp; test/run.f. Depends: the allocator vocabulary dot. Ownership: hazel.
