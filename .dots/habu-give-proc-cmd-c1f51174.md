Problem: lib/process-command.f is declared PROCESS-WIDE (header line 3): the argument and environment tables PROC-CMD-ARG-BUF/ENV-BUF, the capture buffers PROC-CMD-IN/OUT/ERR (128K/32K/32K), PROC-CMD-CWD and the thirteen cursor and outcome cells (lines 44-64) are one set for the image, and the builders below it keep global counters too (lib/process-argv.f PROC-ARGV-N/OFF; lib/process-env.f PROC-ENV-N/OFF/I, PROC-PATH-I, PROC-ENV-DEF-N/OFF, PROC-ENV-CAP-N, PROC-ENV-BUF-CAP-N, PROC-ENV-INHERITED-N). Tasks are pthreads, so a task that prepared a command runs whatever another task prepared since. Found by cedar for Tender (pin 806f0654, engine 274f9bea; PROC-CMD runs its extraction and fill children, a release prerequisite for Tender 7d0da90f). Witnessed reproducer /home/joel/.cache/tender/habu-gaps/process-call-state/repro.f (run.log beside it; run from that frozen root with TMPDIR=/home/joel/.cache/tmp bin/hb --load repro.f): task A RESETs and adds alpha, task B RESETs, adds beta and runs printf, then A runs and its OUT is beta, rc 1. Constraint: process-command.f is loaded by tools/native-build-core.f, and the capture buffers alone exceed a task region (0x10000 bytes), so the state is caller-owned, not task-local, and there is no job-wide lock. Acceptance: a caller-owned command context: a byte-size constant (or a caller-declared record) laying out the argument table, environment table, cwd, stdin, stdout and stderr captures and the outcome cells, and every PROC-CMD public word (RESET, WIPE, ARG+, ENV+, the cwd and stdin setters, ENV-HERMETIC, RUN-OUTCOME, RUN-RC, the OUT, ERR and exit accessors) taking the context as its first parameter with typed effects; the argv and env builders take their counters from the context or as locals rather than from process-wide variables; lib/process.f's task-local capture slots stay where they are; the process-wide words are either removed with the seven PROC-CMD caller files migrated or kept as thin wrappers over one static context that the header and docs/threads.md call single-task; the capture capacities stay unless the context makes them the caller's. Tests: a two-task row in the reproducer's shape (each task prepares and runs its own printf through its own context and reads its own capture) in lib/process-command-test.f or lib/process-task-test.f, run 20 times; the Tender reproducer is the consumer check. Files: lib/process-command.f, lib/process-argv.f, lib/process-env.f, lib/process-command-test.f, docs/threads.md (the lib/process.f row). Verify: the process test files, the seven callers' tests, the chain (loaded by the engine build). Depends: none. Ownership: lib/process-command.f, lib/process-argv.f, lib/process-env.f. Claim: hazel, queued behind the fs dot.
