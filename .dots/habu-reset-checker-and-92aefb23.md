Sixty-one persisted DP-heap cells still hold a dead pointer in every snapshot image. Each one has a named owner now - the owner table below was measured with tools/snap-heap-owner.f (documented in docs/debugging.md) against the cells that differ between two images built from one hb-stdin and one hb-snap-src, so no owner here is a guess. Every one of them is a scratch or cache buffer whose leftover content nothing reads after restore; the treatment the parent dot habu-fix-persisted-dangling-a520f7b4 prescribes for that class is a reset at the owner, in its snapshot-prepare or the equivalent, not a hardcoded offset in the writer. Owners, with the number of drifting cells and what the cells hold. src/core/generated-declaration.f SPAN-BUF, 25 cells, JIT-region addresses in 56-byte records of five. src/habu/treeshake.f REACHBUF, 6 cells, leftover reachability bytes. src/core/sha256.f SHA-IO, 5 cells, the last hashed input still sitting in the buffer. src/core/checker.f PES, 5 cells, source-text mmap pointers and page bases. src/habu/habu2.f FROZEN and the cells just above it, 5 cells. src/core/checker.f FAM-U, 3 cells, consecutive source-text byte pointers. src/core/checker.f EI-AK, 2 cells. src/core/include.f REQUIRE-LENS, 2 cells. src/core/layout-valid.f FIELD-I, 2 cells. the type-family row buffer near TF-RBF-DEPTH, 2 cells. src/core/checker.f CK-USE-NAMES, 1 cell, a region pointer in the using-package mirror, which is empty at rest. src/core/checker.f NDH, 1 cell. src/core/checker.f NORET-BOOT, 1 cell. src/core/lower-cert-base.f BUF-BOOT, 1 cell. Reproduce the list: build two images with 'HB_TMP=<root> hb-stdin --build hb-snap-src -- <root>', take the DATA-payload offsets from 'cmp -l', and pair them against SNAP-HEAP-OWNER:DUMP. Where an owner already has a snapshot-prepare hook - the checker ones reach CHECKER-SNAPSHOT-PREPARE in src/core/checker.f, which already resets TOKBUF, HIDX, the USIGS store and NORET - add the reset there; where there is none, give the owning package one and call it from the same place. Do NOT zero a buffer whose live prefix the image needs; reset the cursor and clear only above it, and prove each one by rebuilding and booting. Acceptance: the two-build DATA compare reports zero differing cells outside the engine-reserved band, and a restored image still checks a definition. Depends on nothing; can land before or after habu-relocate-persisted-region-47de06b9.
