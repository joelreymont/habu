Problem: P6 writes every call and address immediate as a fixed-width padded LEB (5 bytes for a u32, 10 for an s64) so the linker patches it in place, and sizes bodies and sections with minimal LEBs (wasm-backend.md §10.1, §10.2, §17.1). Habu has only private unsigned varints inside baked files (src/habu/cell-grid.f:58-75, src/habu/aot-lib.f:325), no signed or padded form, and nothing a runtime-loaded backend may reopen. Acceptance: package WLEB in src/arch/wasm/leb.f: minimal ULEB32, ULEB64, SLEB32 and SLEB64 writers into a caller's bounded buffer; padded writers of exactly 5 (u32) and 10 (s64) bytes for every value of the width; an in-place padded rewrite that refuses a field that is not a padded LEB of that width; strict readers that refuse overlong, truncated and out-of-range input, and a padded reader for the capture. Rows: 0, 63, 64, 127, 128, 2^32-1, 2^32 refused as u32, -1, -64, -65, MIN-N, MAX-N; padded and minimal round trips; refusals throw WLEB's codes -9800..-9804, the first of the Wasm backend's block -9800..-9829, minted in src/arch/wasm/ (lead correction 2026-10-04: -8800..-8829 lies inside lib/errors.f's reserved compiler range, which tools/error-code-lint.f refuses to other files); span capacity, range and length refusals keep the span package's codes. A call field is the five bytes after the `call`/`call_indirect` opcode and an address field the ten after `i64.const`; the padded reader answers a field at an offset and refuses one that is not a padded LEB of that width. Files: src/arch/wasm/leb.f (new), test/wasm/leb.f (new), test/gate-stdlib-cases.f (SUITE wasm-leb), lib/errors.f (comments only: one line naming the -9800..-9829 block, and the compiler range's unassigned list drops the stale -8880..-8899 that C6XSIM and C6XEABI own). Verify: bin/hb --load test/wasm/leb.f on the product engine; a padded writer mutated to write a minimal LEB fails the in-place rewrite row. Depends: none (habu-add-the-wasm-4c32353e landed).
