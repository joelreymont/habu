Problem: P6 writes every call and address immediate as a fixed-width padded LEB (5 bytes for a u32, 10 for an s64) so the linker patches it in place, and sizes bodies and sections with minimal LEBs (wasm-backend.md §10.1, §10.2, §17.1). Habu has only private unsigned varints inside baked files (src/habu/cell-grid.f:58-75, src/habu/aot-lib.f:325), no signed or padded form, and nothing a runtime-loaded backend may reopen. Acceptance: package WLEB in src/arch/wasm/leb.f: minimal ULEB32, ULEB64, SLEB32 and SLEB64 writers into a caller's bounded buffer; padded writers of exactly 5 (u32) and 10 (s64) bytes for every value of the width; an in-place padded rewrite that refuses a field that is not a padded LEB of that width; strict readers that refuse overlong, truncated and out-of-range input, and a padded reader for the capture. Rows: 0, 63, 64, 127, 128, 2^32-1, 2^32 refused as u32, -1, -64, -65, MIN-N, MAX-N; padded and minimal round trips; refusals throw WLEB's codes -8800..-8804, the first of the Wasm backend's block -8800..-8829. A call field is the five bytes after the `call`/`call_indirect` opcode and an address field the ten after `i64.const`; the padded reader answers a field at an offset and refuses one that is not a padded LEB of that width. Files: src/arch/wasm/leb.f (new), test/wasm/leb.f (new), test/gate-stdlib-cases.f (SUITE wasm-leb), lib/errors.f (one comment line naming the block, errors.f:10-13, :521-525; the same edit drops -8800..-8829 and the stale -8880..-8889 from the unassigned list, which C6XSIM already owns at errors.f:518). Verify: bin/hb --load test/wasm/leb.f on the product engine; a padded writer mutated to write a minimal LEB fails the in-place rewrite row. Depends: none (habu-add-the-wasm-4c32353e landed). Rule: the backend loads at run time on the sealed product engine and uses public words only (portability.md:348); no edit to any sealed package (CTARGET, CBIND, HIR, NBACK, NELAB, NCOMP, NEMIT, NSHADOW, IR-*) after habu-add-the-wasm-4c32353e. Ownership: src/arch/wasm/leb.f, test/wasm/leb.f, the lib/errors.f comment line. Lane: tim. Claim: unassigned.
