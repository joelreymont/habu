Problem: product-field transactions are mutated through raw global PF-BEGIN, PF-ADD, PF-PUBLISH, PF-COMMIT, PF-FINALIZE, and PF-ROLLBACK words. The event participant cannot adopt a qualified owner without leaving duplicate authority or changing its direct callers. The rejected combined publish-and-release replacement is incorrect for the global declaration coordinator: a later participant can fail after fields commit, so the field frame must retain rollback authority until global finalization.

Exact contract: make package TYPE-FIELD-OWNER the sole lifecycle authority. It publicly owns OPEN, ADD, PREPARE, COMMIT, FINALIZE, PUBLISH, and ROLLBACK. OPEN snapshots PF-N, TF-STR-U, and PF-COMMIT-N in one strict-LIFO frame. ADD accepts only that frame's exact live token and open state. PREPARE validates the token, open state, and saved/live watermarks without mutation. COMMIT accepts the exact open frame, advances PF-COMMIT-N only for the outer frame, marks the frame committed, and retains it. FINALIZE accepts only that committed frame and releases it. ROLLBACK accepts an open or committed frame, scrubs its provisional rows, restores all three saved watermarks including PF-COMMIT-N, and then releases it. PUBLISH is the one-shot PREPARE, COMMIT, FINALIZE composition used only by the legacy PRODUCT path after its last fallible step. Every phase validates before mutation; stale tokens and wrong states leave all watermarks and depth unchanged.

Remove every raw global PF lifecycle entry point without forwarding aliases. Migrate src/core/sumtype.f to one-shot PUBLISH. Migrate DECL-EVENT so its PREPARE, COMMIT, FINALIZE, and ROLLBACK delegate the matching field-owner phase; it must not keep a second field snapshot or release the field frame during COMMIT. In type-family-suite.f and type-family-rollback-suite.f, move only changed transaction shims into short test packages and qualify callers. Put absence and hostile-reopen tests in one focused package-owned test module; revert unrelated internal-word-gate additions. Update existing TRUSTED rows without adding a boundary.

Acceptance: inject a later declaration participant COMMIT failure through the real DECLARATION-TRANSACTION runner and real event callbacks; the event and field published counts, provisional counts, saved string watermark, and all transaction depths must return to their exact prior values. Prove PREPARE is byte-for-byte non-mutating, COMMIT retains rollback authority, FINALIZE alone releases committed state, ROLLBACK restores open and committed frames, legacy PUBLISH remains atomic, nested inner success followed by outer rollback restores both frames, and stale/wrong-state tokens reject before mutation. The production product declaration, declaration-event, type-family, rollback, generated-declaration, structure, and enum suites pass. Checked definitions through the production checker reject every retired PF lifecycle name; interpreter dictionary absence is supplemental only. A hostile package reopen cannot reach private lifecycle helpers. Package-diff lint reports zero without exemptions.
