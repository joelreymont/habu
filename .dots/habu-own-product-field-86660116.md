Problem: product-field transactions are mutated through raw global PF-BEGIN, PF-ADD, PF-PUBLISH, PF-COMMIT, PF-FINALIZE, and PF-ROLLBACK words. The event participant cannot adopt a qualified owner without leaving duplicate authority or changing its direct callers. The rejected combined publish-and-release replacement is incorrect for the global declaration coordinator: a later participant can fail after fields commit, so the field frame must retain rollback authority until global finalization.

Exact contract: make package TYPE-FIELD-OWNER the sole lifecycle authority. It publicly owns OPEN, ADD, PREPARE, COMMIT, FINALIZE, and ROLLBACK; no one-shot PUBLISH surface remains. OPEN snapshots PF-N, TF-STR-U, and PF-COMMIT-N in one strict-LIFO frame. At nonzero depth, OPEN first requires the live parent to remain open with valid marks; a committed parent rejects before token generation, capacity growth, or mutation and remains finalizable or rollbackable. ADD accepts only that frame's exact live token and open state. PREPARE validates the token, open state, and saved/live watermarks without mutation. COMMIT accepts the exact open frame, advances PF-COMMIT-N only for the outer frame, marks the frame committed, and retains it. FINALIZE accepts only that committed frame and releases it. ROLLBACK accepts an open or committed frame, scrubs its provisional rows, restores all three saved watermarks including PF-COMMIT-N, and then releases it. Every phase validates before mutation; stale tokens and wrong states leave token generation, all watermarks, and depth unchanged.

Remove every raw global PF lifecycle entry point and the obsolete field-owner PUBLISH path without forwarding aliases. Migrate DECL-EVENT so its PREPARE, COMMIT, FINALIZE, and ROLLBACK delegate the matching field-owner phase; it must not keep a second field snapshot or release the field frame during COMMIT. PRODUCT must not open or publish a second field transaction. Its existing TDECL-PRODUCT-TX runs through the installed TDECL-TXN-XT coordinator even when CHECKER-DEFPRODUCT is called directly by CHECK or VERIFY; inside that exact transaction it obtains DECL-EVENT:CURRENT, emits DECL and ARITY, and routes every field through DECL-EVENT:FIELD. Public PRODUCT therefore nests safely inside its existing outer generated-declaration transaction, while direct metadata replay gains the same event and field owner. The outer coordinator alone prepares, commits, finalizes, or rolls back the field frame. Optional legacy POLICY and DERIVE parsing remains existing family metadata in this leaf; its removal belongs to the unified STRUCTURE cutover, not field lifecycle ownership. In type-family-suite.f and type-family-rollback-suite.f, move only changed transaction shims into short test packages and qualify callers. Put absence and hostile-reopen tests in one focused package-owned test module; revert unrelated internal-word-gate additions. Update existing TRUSTED rows without adding a boundary.

Acceptance: inject a later declaration participant COMMIT failure through the real DECLARATION-TRANSACTION runner and real event callbacks; the event and field published counts, provisional counts, saved string watermark, and all transaction depths must return to their exact prior values. Prove PREPARE is byte-for-byte non-mutating, COMMIT retains rollback authority, FINALIZE alone releases committed state, ROLLBACK restores open and committed frames, nested inner success followed by outer rollback restores both frames, and stale/wrong-state tokens reject before mutation. Through the public API, commit an outer frame, reject nested OPEN with unchanged token generation, depth, provisional and published counts, and string watermark, then prove the same outer token still finalizes and still rolls back in independent cases. A public PRODUCT and direct CHECKER-DEFPRODUCT replay each publish one DECL, one ARITY, and exactly one FIELD event per field; zero-field and injected field failure roll back every family, event, field, schema, and string watermark; removing the nested coordinator or routing any field directly to TYPE-FIELD-OWNER makes the production suites fail. The production product declaration, CHECK, VERIFY, declaration-event, type-family, rollback, generated-declaration, structure, and enum suites pass. Checked definitions through the production checker reject every retired PF lifecycle name, including PUBLISH; interpreter dictionary absence is supplemental only. A hostile package reopen cannot reach private lifecycle helpers. Package-diff lint reports zero without exemptions.
