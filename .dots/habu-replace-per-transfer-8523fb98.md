Problem: cedar's 2026-09-14 series (dcd369bf, 61b07c76, 7be9b152, 6739c962) checks the active stack allocation's bounds at every native transfer and after every call, in every primitive and at every JIT push: an 11-instruction frame plus a call into a check routine that saves and restores NZCV. The pinned engine carries 117,716 such sequences, 5.2 MB of its 7.4 MB of code (7.5 per word), every primitive lost inlining, tier-1 builds of the core prefix take 213 s, and the boot seed resolves the helper's name once per guard site. The hole the series closed is real (epic habu-bound-engine-stacks-804cf613: fixed stack regions inside the DATA header overflow into neighbouring cells with no diagnostic; run-in-stack ignored its size), but it is a capacity problem, not a per-access one. Acceptance: no guard sequences in compiled code (NWALK:GUARDS reports 0 everywhere, STACK-GUARD:CHECK-DATA/RETURN/LOOP and JIT-STACK:CHECK-* and PUT-STACK-GUARD are gone with their callers, mirrors in bootstrap/cg removed); each stack region (data, return, loop) sits on its own page-aligned extent with an inaccessible page beyond it, and the crash handler names the stack on the fault (data/return/loop) with the existing STACK-BOUNDS exit; the interpreter keeps its depth floor, LARITY and E-UNDERFLOW; catch/evaluate/task/REPL recovery keep their descriptor checks at the switch only; run-in-stack takes a stack allocated by a new word that maps it with its guard page and refuses a plain buffer (E-STACK-UNGUARDED); test/engine-stack-lifecycle.f and test/bootstrap-engine-stack.fs rewritten for faults instead of checks, cedar's native-stack-contract.f/native-stack-bounds.f retired, guard-aware fixtures keep their NWALK readers at 0; engine rebuilt from the tree to a byte fixpoint and the full gate rerun. Files: src/habu/rt.f, src/habu/jit.f, src/habu/habu1.f, src/habu/habu2.f, src/habu/layout.f, src/habu/stack-abi.f, src/habu/crash.f, src/compiler/native/emit.f, src/compiler/native/regalloc-verify.f, src/compiler/native/select.f, bootstrap/cg/forth.fs, bootstrap/cg/jit.fs, the fixtures above.
