Problem: Maki currently registers cubin paths with `MDL-CUBIN!`, loads them later, records device verdict tags, and promotes rows without one typed subject binding the exact cubin/SASS payload through launch and evidence. A path swap, one-byte mutation, wrong region/symbol/ABI, stale launch result, or evidence from another cubin can therefore preserve host types and verdict values. Fix: add package `CUBIN-INTEGRITY` with an immutable artifact handle indexed by target, toolchain, verified PTX digest, `ptxas` attestation digest, cubin payload digest, kernel symbol, ABI schema, and verifier version. Re-hash bytes at registration and immediately before module load; bind launch configuration/environment to the handle; emit device semantic evidence over that exact subject; require the same subject through report, store, replay, and promotion. Optional SASS/disassembly evidence is content-bound and policy-indexed; if policy requires independent SASS evidence, a missing or unsupported verifier rejects rather than weakening the claim. The generic artifact envelope and proof-obligation dots remain their sole schema/policy owners; `habu-make-ptx-device-c0eb12a3` and `habu-committed-device-correctness-9ca4cbc6` remain device-execution and golden-suite owners; `habu-emit-proof-carrying-058f43b6` remains Habu-owned allocation-certificate owner. Acceptance negatives: cubin byte mutation after attestation or registration, swapped region artifact, target/toolchain/PTX/attestation mismatch, wrong symbol or ABI, changed grid/block/shared-memory configuration, stale environment, stale or wrong-subject golden/profile evidence, missing required SASS verifier, and promotion with only verdict tags all reject before launch or promotion with structured subject diagnostics. Acceptance positives: one exact sm_87 cubin flows through typed registration, module load, launch, nonuniform device golden, persistent replay, and evidence-gated promotion; an independently reproduced digest/evidence envelope verifies after restart. Ownership: new `lib/ptx/cubin-integrity.f`, `lib/ptx/cubin-integrity-test.f`, `maki/evidence/device-code.f`, and `maki/evidence/device-code-test.f`; typed-handle migrations in `maki/lower-launch.f`, `maki/lower-golden.f`, `maki/lower-model-device-test.f`, `maki/report.f`, `maki/cad.f`, `maki/store.f`, `maki/lower-model-test.f`, `maki/report-test.f`, `maki/cad-test.f`, and `maki/store-test.f`; required manifest and `FILEMAP.md` rows. Device tools and kernel goldens are read-only consumers except for the minimal typed-handle adapter; no new CUDA rc, golden, generic artifact, proof-policy, resource-model, or allocation-certificate logic. Verify: mutation/swap/staleness matrix, report/store/replay/promotion subject tests, off-device negative suite, Orin launch and committed device goldens, Maki full suite, `ptx-stdlib`, typed-local-diff-lint, trust lint, host-lint, filemap-lint, native fixpoint and full native/device gates.
