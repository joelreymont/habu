Problem: `PTXTC:ASSEMBLE` and `tools/ptx/ptxas-smoke.f` prove only process success and a nonempty cubin. They do not bind the exact verified PTX, target, resolved `ptxas` identity/configuration, invocation, diagnostics, resource report, and output bytes. Proprietary `ptxas` allocation and scheduling cannot receive a Habu allocation certificate and must not be represented as statically proved. Fix: add package `PTXAS-ATTEST` that accepts only PTX carrying `PTX-STATE` verification evidence, resolves the immutable toolchain descriptor owned by `habu-v2-r3-define-987402c7`, runs the exact target/configured assembler, captures diagnostics and versioned resource output, hashes canonical input and output bytes, parses typed register/shared/local/constant/stack/spill facts, and emits an artifact-indexed opaque-backend attestation under the generic proof-obligation policy. The attestation proves exact provenance, successful assembly, report well-formedness, and resource facts; it explicitly does not prove proprietary allocation or scheduling semantics. `habu-v2-resource-model-985a0b0e` remains sole owner of predicted-versus-attested calibration and consumes this API. Acceptance negatives: unverified or mutated PTX, target mismatch, toolchain/config/version drift, missing executable, nonzero/timeout result, malformed/duplicate/unknown required report field, report-output mismatch, stale resource attestation, absent cubin, and a backend without the required verification policy all fail closed with structured diagnostics. Acceptance positives: a deterministic fixture report and the sm_87 SAXPY assembly bind the exact PTX, toolchain, target, argv policy, resource row, cubin digest, and verifier version; replay verifies without trusting process memory or paths. Ownership: new `lib/ptx/ptxas-attest.f`, `lib/ptx/ptxas-attest-test.f`, `maki/evidence/ptxas.f`, and `maki/evidence/ptxas-test.f`; assembly-boundary migrations in `tools/ptx/ptxas-smoke.f`, `maki/lower/device-test.f`, `maki/lower/mm-device-test.f`, `maki/lower/red-device-test.f`, `maki/lower/mv-device-test.f`, `maki/lower/model-device-test.f`, `maki/ablate-golden-device-test.f`, and `maki/precision-device-test.f`. `lib/ptx/toolchain.f` and `maki/target/toolchain.f` are dependency seams owned by the R3 toolchain dot, not reimplemented here. Excludes resource estimation/calibration, Habu-owned register-allocation certificates, cubin launch identity, semantic device correctness, and promotion policy. Verify: report parser mutation matrix, deterministic replay/hash fixtures, missing/stale toolchain cases, Orin `ptxas` smoke, Maki device assembly slice, typed-local-diff-lint, native fixpoint and full native gate.
