Location: stdlib.habu as macro or compile.zig
Syntax:
  (prog ((var init)*) [tag | statement]*)

Examples:
  (prog ((x 0))
    loop
    (if (> x 5) (return x))
    (incf x)
    (go loop))
Expands to: (block nil (let (...) (tagbody ...)))
