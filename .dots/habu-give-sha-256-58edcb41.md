Problem: src/core/sha256.f keeps every SHA-256 state cell process-wide (H, WS, SHA-TAIL, SHA-TAIL-U, SHA-TOTAL, PBLK and the SHA-A/SHA-P/SHA-SRC/SHA-DST/SHA-OUT/SHA-BLOCK-A argument holders), so SHA256-RESET/UPDATE/FINAL and SHA256>HEX are one shared digest per process: two interleaved digests corrupt each other, and tasks are pthreads (docs/threads.md), so even one block is a race. lib/crypto/evp.f offers only one-shot HMAC-SHA256/HMAC-SHA1, no streaming digest. Found by cedar for Tender LLM call-state isolation (Tender pin 806f0654, engine 274f9bea): callbacks need independent digests passable between them, not a process lock. Witnessed reproducer: /home/joel/.cache/tender/habu-gaps/sha-call-state/repro.f (run.log beside it; frozen root .jj-ws/tender-806f0654): task A resets and updates alpha, task B resets, updates and finalizes beta, then A finalizes and differs from its serial alpha digest, exit 1. Acceptance: a caller-owned context: a byte-size constant and typed words to init a context span, add a span to it and finish it into a 32-byte span (names per the card); every mutable cell (H, tail, tail count, total, message schedule, pad block) lives in the context or in typed locals, no process-wide cell is read or written on the streaming path, and the context is a plain span a caller may hand between callbacks and threads. SHA256, SHA256-FILE, SHA256-FILE-HEX and SHA256>HEX keep their effects on top of the context words; the process-wide RESET/UPDATE/FINAL trio is either removed with its callers migrated (src/habu/aot-file.f, aot-ident.f, aot-owned-cells.f, tools/diff-side-content.f, diff-side-content-read.f, diff-side-content-test.f, test/aot-owned.f, aot-owned-capture.f, aot-artifact-rows.f, tools/sha256-file-test.f) or kept as a thin wrapper over one static context; SHA256-SNAPSHOT-PREPARE shrinks to what remains. The file words too (cedar, for independent fill outputs): SHA256-FILE and SHA256-FILE-HEX share SHA-FD, SHA-IO, SHA-RD and SHA-DIGEST, so worker 2 also makes them reentrant — they hash through a caller-owned context, take the read buffer and digest span from the caller or as locals, and no process-wide cell remains on that path. Tests: a row that interleaves two contexts update by update and matches both against the one-shot digest, and a row with two tasks hashing concurrently into distinct contexts; the Tender reproducer is the consumer-side check. Files: src/core/sha256.f, tools/sha256-file-test.f (or a core test beside it), docs/crypto.md line 7. Verify: the test file, the callers' tests, the chain (engine change). Depends: none.
