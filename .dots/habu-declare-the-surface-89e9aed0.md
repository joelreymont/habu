Problem: the engine image carries 4,753 global and 2,695 package-public records with names, checker signatures and symbols (docs/engine-size.md, 2026-09-16), most of them compiler and checker plumbing that is global or public only because src/core/checker.f and src/habu/habu2.f predate packages (checker.f calls itself legacy global surface). The private-stripping children (habu-seal-every-captured-c550102f, habu-drop-private-signatures-974304d0) do not touch them, so the words a REPL user can name, and whose signatures the image must keep, are still undefined; today the seal (src/core/internal-mark.f) marks a hand-classified set DNAME-INT and everything else stays reachable and typed. Joel (2026-09-16): only the code required to operate the AOT compiler, JIT and REPL belongs in the binary, and no package or type signature unless those require it. Acceptance: (1) the surface is declared once, as a named list in engine source (the primitives, the loader and REPL words, the compiler entry points hb-build and the tools call, the debugger commands), each entry with a one-line reason, and a lint refuses an engine build whose global or package-public word is neither on the list nor DNAME-INT; (2) at the seal every global and package-public word of the captured engine that is not on the list is marked internal exactly like today's DNAME-INT set (not executable, not tickable, not resolvable from user source), so a user who needs one gets E-UNDEFINED and forks the source; (3) at capture every internal word loses its record, name, signature and symbol like a private word (extend habu-ship-no-dictionary-2fee2dea and habu-drop-private-signatures-974304d0 to the internal set: one rule, sealed-internal, for both); tools that need internals read the sidecar or run on the unsealed engine; (4) engine-size before and after, with the record and signature counts by class, the full gate, the stripped-application suites, and Radar, Tender, Loom and Maki green (their launchers and tools audited for internal names first, each hit made public on the list with a reason or converted); (5) docs/forth.md states what the surface is and where the list lives.
