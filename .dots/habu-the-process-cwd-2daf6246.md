Fix in work (/Users/joel/Work/reviews/habu-10x-2026-11-10/briefs8/Fix-process-cwd-early-eof-test.md): the deadline captures claim the timeout, the cleared pid and stdin, and a captured stdout that is a prefix of 'ok'; a third capture under a 60 s guard claims 'ok' and a clean exit; PCT-OUT is cleared before each. The hunt also names a window in the capture loops of src/lib/process.f (bytes that land between the last poll and the deadline check are not read). Settled by the long-term rule: not a defect. No deadline can promise bytes a child writes at the deadline: a final zero-wait drain narrows the window and cannot close it (the child may write between that drain and the kill), and a caller cannot tell a byte lost there from one written a microsecond after the deadline. The loops' stated contract (one ready read per stream survives an expired deadline) holds.
