Problem: docs/x86-64.md 'The emitter's sink' decided emit-x64.f (X64EMIT) is a separate emitter over the lib/byte-buffer.f BUF sink, layout in bytes, rel32 branches, MOVABS sites; nothing of it exists. src/arch/x86-64/asm.f already has every encoder the 36 opcodes the selector reaches need (ENC-MOV-RI64 with the imm at MOV-RI64-IMM-OFF 2, ENC-MOV-RR, the five tied -RR forms and their -RI32 twins, ENC-IMUL-RR, ENC-SHL/SHR-RI8, ENC-CMP-RR/-RI32 + ENC-SETCC + ENC-MOVZX-8-RR, ENC-TEST-RR + ENC-JCC-REL32, ENC-JMP-REL32, ENC-CALL-REL32, ENC-RET, ENC-MOV-MR/-RM with MEM-OFF/MEM-AT/MEM-IDX/MEM-RIP, ENC-MOVZX-8-RM, ENC-MOV8-MR, ENC-SUB/ADD-RI32) but no size query (an instruction is sized by encoding it into a scratch BUF) and no decoder; X64IR keeps the data-stack pointer register private (R-DSP = r12, x64ir.f ~176) and the emitter needs it public (X64IR:DSTACK-GPR or X64M:DSTACK-GPR, as A64M:DSTACK-GPR is). Acceptance, the smallest slice that yields pinned bytes: X64EMIT for a STRAIGHT-LINE, SINGLE-BLOCK, NO-CALL, NO-FRAME allocated module - movi, mov, the five tied binaries and their *i forms, shli/shri, not, cmpset/cmpseti (cmp + setcc + movzx), dtake/dload/dstore/dpublish against the data-stack register, aload/astore/abload/abstore, ret - registers read through the one door A64RAV:REG@ (as emit.f does), slot/frame attributes read off the module; no branch layout, no rel32, no relocation, no NPUB: the test reads the BUF and pins the bytes of a two-value leaf against llvm-mc vectors the way test/compiler/x86-64-asm.f does. Files: src/compiler/native/emit-x64.f, src/compiler/native/x64ir.f (DSTACK-GPR), test/compiler/x64-emit.f, lib/errors.f (-8780..-8799 reserved for X64EMIT). Verify: the suite; three generations with cmp; test/run.f. Depends: the allocator vocabulary dot (an allocated X64IR module). Ownership: hazel. Claim: agent=hazel workspace=.jj-ws/hazel-x64-emit.

Audit note (d5847859): ENC-MOV32-MI32 takes a RIP displacement relative to the instruction END, imm32 included, and the encoder exposes no length helper for it; the emitter computes that fixup from the length it emits.
