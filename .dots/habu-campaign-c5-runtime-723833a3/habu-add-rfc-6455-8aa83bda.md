Problem: Maki streams meshes to a browser viewer and Habu has no WebSocket (maki docs/habu-needs.md). The handshake needs SHA-1 and base64: lib/crypto/evp.f:67,:333 has only HMAC-SHA-1 through libcrypto, the engine digest is SHA-256 (src/core/sha256.f), lib/ has no base64. The HTTP worker serves a connection request by request (tender server/http.f:243-256) and cannot give it to an upgrade handler. Design: lib/net/ws.f, package WS: `WS:ACCEPT ( HTTP:request HTTP:response -- WS:accepted )` inside a route handler checks an HTTP/1.1 GET with one Host, Upgrade, Connection, Sec-WebSocket-Version 13 and Key, sets TCP_NODELAY, answers 101 with Sec-WebSocket-Accept and answers `open` with the socket, which the handler owns until it returns; a refused handshake is answered 400 or 426 through the response and `ACCEPT` answers `refused` with that status, minting no socket. `WS:RECEIVE ( WS:socket ms -- WS:message )` with text, binary, closed and timeout arms waits at most its absolute wait (a control-frame answer may add WS:STALL-MS and one send slice), reassembles continuations, answers ping (also while closing), closes 1002 on a frame the codec refuses or a broken sequence, 1007 on invalid UTF-8 (lib/utf8-scalar.f), 1009 past MESSAGE-MAX, and on HTTP:STOP sends close 1001 and answers closed 1001, so the worker ends without a kill. `WS:SEND-TEXT` (refusing non-UTF-8 with E-WS-TEXT), `WS:SEND-BINARY`, `WS:CLOSE ( WS:socket n -- )`; sends are serialised by a TASK:FACILITY per worker slot so a push from another task interleaves whole frames, and every write is bounded: WS:STALL-MS for frames (a peer that takes no byte for it fails the socket under 1006), HTTP:RELEASE-MS for the worker's goodbye, and a worker letting its socket go, retiring it, or closing it on a stop cuts a push that has moved no byte for RELEASE-MS. A task inside a send is joined, never killed. The frame codec (lib/net/ws-frame.f), SHA-1 (lib/crypto/sha1.f) and base64 (lib/base64.f) need no HTTP and land first. Decided: pure-Habu SHA-1 and base64, no libcrypto in the server path; the upgraded socket lives in its HTTP worker for its life for the first landing; no ping word and no fragmented sends until a caller needs them. Acceptance: lib/net/ws-test.f: a Habu client over TCP4 handshakes (RFC 6455 section 1.3 key answers s3pPLMBiTxaQ9kYGzzhZRbK+xOo=); every malformed handshake, HTTP/1.0, no Host and two Hosts are refused with the handler seeing 400 or 426; echoes text, binary at each length form's edges up to 70000 bytes, a fragmented message, ping/pong, each failure status, a close handshake from either end with a pong while closing, a push from a second task; a send through a handle past its socket or past a killed worker is E-WS-CLOSED; a peer that stops reading fails the socket under 1006 once STALL-MS passes, not before; a stop ends a worker in RECEIVE with close 1001 and no kill, also behind another task's stalled push and when its own pong stalls; a goodbye behind a stalled push ends without a kill; a burst of 4000 pings cannot hold a 50 ms RECEIVE; text that is not UTF-8 is refused before anything is written; artifact build/ws-transcript.txt.
