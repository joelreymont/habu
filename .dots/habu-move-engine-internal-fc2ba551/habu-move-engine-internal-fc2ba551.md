Problem: engine-internal primitives (trust-sig!, body-append, def-open, namespace-record, int-mark, ndict-append, ffi-call, set-check, ...) sit in the global dictionary. The product keeps them and refuses them only by marks (measured on the installed hb: `' trust-sig!` -> "E-UNSAFE habu: ...: internal engine word: trust-sig!", `' ffi-call` -> "E-CAP-TRUSTED habu: ...: trusted-only tick: ffi-call"). A per-primitive owner table bolts caller checks on top: src/habu/prims.f EPPRIM: (53 rows; xref-search-wl has 5 owner rows). It still leaks: on the installed hb `package OUTER public : EVIL ( ptr u8 n -- ) body-append ; ;package` certifies. Package privacy gives the restriction once the build strips private names (habu-re-enable-hb-a2404c26): a qualified private name such as XREF:N>REC is already E-UNDEFINED and public XREF:REC resolves, but hb keeps every private name today.
Ruling (Joel, 2026-10-08): system words go in the system package's private section, never the global dictionary; no owner rows, no caller checks. Restricting a word = putting it in a private section.
Acceptance: every engine-internal primitive is a private word of the system package; EPPRIM:/ECLOSE-PRIVATE and the K-PKG-PRIVATE row kind are deleted; a primitive several packages used gets one home and the others call its public word (pattern: XREF:WL-RECORD over xref-search-wl); on the product `' trust-sig!` and the OUTER squat above answer E-UNDEFINED; the engine's own callers are compiled before the strip and keep working.
Measure first: how the engine's packages name a system-private word while the engine is built. The strip is habu-re-enable-hb-a2404c26, built and measured beside this work; it lands as this work's last step.
Supersedes the owner-row design of habu-honour-owner-private-0a19f45d.
Files: src/habu/prims.f, src/core/checker.f (K-PKG-PRIVATE in NAV-PRIM-ROW), src/habu/primitive-registry.f, the callers in src/habu/*.f and src/core/*.f.
Verify: product probe of the refused names above; full suite on the built hb.
Depends: none. Ownership: lead. Claim: unassigned.
