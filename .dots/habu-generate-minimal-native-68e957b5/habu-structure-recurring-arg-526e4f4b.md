Invariant: a recurring group of same-width values with stable semantic roles is one typed value; its correctness does not depend on stack position or a handwritten arity threshold. A repository-wide signature census finds many seven-to-twelve-cell interfaces. Some are legitimate short-lived independent inputs, but confirmed repeated aggregates include binary segment and section metadata, multi-tensor forward and backward state, repeated path components, report-capture configuration, object source identity, and PTY resources. Same-cell permutations currently certify and deep local binders repeat at every call.

Build a tokenizer-aware signature and call-site census, then classify each high-arity interface by semantic recurrence, role ambiguity, and lifetime rather than number alone. Exclude quotations whose apparent arity belongs to nested effects and exclude interfaces already owned by exact structure dots. For each confirmed aggregate, create a small domain-owned leaf that introduces nominal field roles and STRUCTURE values, constructs once at the validation boundary, threads the value through consumers, and deletes positional compatibility APIs. Keep ordinary stack parameters when they are genuinely independent and clearer.

The existing PTY-authority and object-source dots are concrete leaves and must not be duplicated. Prove every migrated pairwise same-width field swap fails checking, construct and destruct round trips, wire or ABI layout parity where externally fixed, ownership and lifetime transitions, exact behavioral goldens, and focused performance. Add a checked report that prevents new unclassified high-arity recurring aggregates without enforcing a magic maximum. Verify typed-local, structure, package, trust, host, file-map, Maki, PTX, bootstrap, fixpoint, and full native gates. Measure stack depth, locals, definitions, source, JIT, DATA, CODELEN, and hot-path latency before and after; require structural safety and no unexplained growth.
