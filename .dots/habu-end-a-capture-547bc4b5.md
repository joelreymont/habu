Problem (tim, from Maki, 2026-11-07; reproducer /Users/joel/Work/maki/.jj-ws.noindex/tim/.out/carl/kill-capture/README.md): src/lib/process.f KILL-CAPTURE (:509-513) and a capture's deadline end (:36-40, 'killed its child's tree with SIGKILL') SIGKILL the child's whole tree, so a root that cleans up on SIGTERM never runs its exit path: test/db/pg-cluster.f ANSWER (:367-382) stops PostgreSQL there, and a SIGKILLed postgres leaves its SysV segment (kern.sysv.shmmni is 32 here) and its habu-pg-* directories for good. Maki's runner (all-tests.f STOPPED :331, Maki 9072dbdb) and tools/check-core.f CHK-SIGNAL-ANSWER (:1952-1955) both reach it. test/gate-pool.f already does it right privately (:504-583: GT-POOL-ASK-END / GT-POOL-AWAIT-END send SIGTERM only to a root that catches it, PROC-TREE:CATCHES?, wait until it exits or a grace bound passes, then kill the tree; a root that does not catch SIGTERM is killed at once, since the default action would orphan what it spawned). Design (lead's decision under Joel's night words of 2026-11-06, record in DECISIONS.md at landing): KILL-CAPTURE's own behaviour becomes that, and the deadline end uses the same path; no new public word (kill(2) and kill(1) also default to asking with SIGTERM); the grace bound is a private constant of PROCESS; gate-pool.f's private copy goes and calls the library. Acceptance: a capture whose child catches SIGTERM and removes a marker file in its handler, ended by KILL-CAPTURE and by a deadline, leaves no marker (fails today); a child that ignores SIGTERM still ends within the bound; a tree whose root does not catch SIGTERM ends at once as today; gate-pool's pg rows unchanged.
