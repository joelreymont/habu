Problem: src/lib/byte-buffer.f makes callers reserve a raw three-cell header (create X BUF:HDR-BYTES allot, HDR-BYTES sums in hand-laid layouts, TASK:+USER and SCRATCH-ALLOT byte counts), against docs/design.md:19-21 typed containers; Maki's first typed-container site (maki-hold-a-project-70f4b714) waits. Joel 2026-11-13 named the type BUF:buffer. Joel, 2026-11-19, asked which type Tender's growable byte buffers are, chose VEC:vector<u8> over BUF and asked whether BUF can be merged into the vector instead of kept; no design for that is approved yet, so nothing below is built until he has seen one. Checked that day on hb a45a6990: an engine built with src/lib/vector.f in ENGINE-LIB-FILES before byte-buffer.f builds (native; Wasm not tried); BUF's append, byte push, span and clear written over a vector<u8> ran a million 43-byte appends in 0.14 s on the heap allocator against BUF's 0.19 s; a vector in an arena peaks at twice its bytes, since the arena keeps each outgrown block until release. What a merge must carry from BUF: a header that lives inside its holder and is empty when zeroed (vector's is allocated by NEW), an append whose source may lie in the buffer's own old block (byte-buffer.f APPEND-SPAN-RAW copies before it releases), and a clear that does not zero the bytes. Acceptance: BUF exports the record type BUF:buffer; INIT, DISPOSE and every BUF word take ptr BUF:buffer; BUF:HDR-BYTES is gone; every Habu caller (52 files: src, tools, test incl. test/xmodem.py, test/serial-xmodem.py) holds its header in a typed container; the engine builds and the suite passes; Zen, Tender and Maki owners are sent the cutover.
