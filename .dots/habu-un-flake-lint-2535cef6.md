Problem: test/lint-cli-standalone-load.f:41 sets TIMEOUT-MS 20000 and LOADS (line 117) asserts 'EXITED @ TTRUE' for every tools/<name>-lint.f entry. STORE! maps a timeout to EXITED=FALSE, so a child that merely ran out of wall clock is reported exactly like a child that died - the assertion cannot tell the two apart. tools/refine-lint.f is the heavy entry and it is an outlier, not merely heavy: measured on 2026-08-04, near-idle, it takes 17.35/17.35/17.47s standalone - 87% of its own budget before any concurrency - against 2.36s for tools/error-code-lint.f and 0.60s for tools/namespace-lint.f over the same 1400-file tree, a 7.7x gap for a comparable whole-tree scan. Under load (average 10) it measured 21.4s and 26.6s, straight through the 20s budget. That is the mechanism behind SUITE stdlib-process-fixtures flaking red under the pool while every member is green standalone: reproduced 2 red in 4 runs of the six-member load line while three full gates ran concurrently, both reds being 'TFAIL assert 17 tools/refine-lint.f / expected true got false'. Not the tty/inherited-stdin class - all three named members already spawn through RUN-ARGV-STDIN-CAPTURE-OUTCOME with an explicit stdin, and each times identically from a tty and from a pipe. Acceptance: a timeout is reported AS a timeout (its own diagnostic naming the entry and the elapsed budget), never folded into 'did not exit'; and the per-child budget is derived rather than a guessed constant - either the entry stops costing 18s (tools/refine-lint-core.f SCAN-ROOT is 7.7x its peers over the same tree, so the first question is what it does per file that they do not), or the fixture's budget is stated against a measured baseline with headroom for a saturated gate. Do not simply raise 20000 to a luckier number. Files: test/lint-cli-standalone-load.f (TIMEOUT-MS, STORE!, LOADS, REFUSES), tools/refine-lint.f, tools/refine-lint-core.f (SCAN-ROOT walks lib/, tools/, test/). Evidence that this is not caused by any recent landing: with the two files added by habu-steady-the-pre-78619720 moved aside, the same command still measures 17.40/17.44s. Two of three clean full-gate runs on 2026-08-04 went red on it. Verify: bin/hb --load tools/refine-lint.f timed idle and under load; the six-member line 'bin/hb --load tools/hb-cli-contracts-test.f tools/standalone-load-test.f test/lint-cli-standalone-load.f lib/process-test.f lib/process-command-test.f lib/process-pty-handle-test.f' green three times while a full gate-stdlib --pool-slots 3 runs alongside.
