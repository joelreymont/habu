Problem: a scoped byte span handed to a quotation is advisory today - the checker cannot bind a pointer to the region that produced it, so a body can stash the span in a ptr field and read it after the owner is released, which is a proven crash after RELEASE. Citing modules that document this exact gap and wait on it: maki/infer/safetensors.f (header lines 51-60: WITH-MAPPING span escape is refused by interface, not proof; lines 738-739 and 900-902: WITH-TENSOR and copy spans equally advisory), the pending WSTORE lane maki/infer/weight-store.f in .jj-ws/habu-wstore (lines 50 and 345-346: the WITH-SLOT crossing is advisory for the same reason), and the create-region gap recorded at the decl-diagnostics delivery: create-region spans are not typed ptr u8 in checked bodies, so three TRUSTED words in the capture helper plus the structure-decl PEND store and fetch pair all document the same missing typed-storage capability. Required capability: the checker forbids a span type produced inside a WITH- combinator from escaping the effect of the quotation (the same class as the release-inventory quotation-result rule), and lets checked bodies type region-backed storage so the TRUSTED boundaries above become checked. Acceptance: a minimal checked fixture that stashes a WITH- span into a variable or ptr field and uses it after the scope is rejected by the checker with a negative regression; the named TRUSTED boundaries in the citing modules are reduced or removed; existing legitimate consumers still certify. Files: src/core/checker.f and the citing modules when the boundaries retire. Verify: checker suites plus the safetensors and weight-store suites. Depends: none. Ownership: checker region and lifetime typing only. Claim: unassigned.
