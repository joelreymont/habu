Problem: tools/jitdump.f (decoder JIT-DIS:STEP, tools/jitdump-arm.f / src/arch/arm64/disasm.f) printed ? for 64 of the 178 instructions in six tier-0/tier-1 listings (2026-10-23, FIB/SUMSQ/MIX in ~/.cache/tmp/jerry-tiers/): bl, b.cond, str/ldr pre- and post-index, ldur/stur, ldp/stp, lsl, madd, and immediate. Decoded operands print as bare numbers, one per line, with no register names or branch targets. llvm-objdump decodes them all. Acceptance: every encoding src/arch/arm64/asm.f can produce decodes to its mnemonic, registers and immediates, one line per instruction with branch targets as offsets; a native-suite row decodes each encoder's output and compares it with what was encoded (the encoder is the oracle, not llvm). Verify: the row; jitdump on the three words matches llvm-objdump. Claim: unassigned.

Acceptance case 2026-11-10 (bob; merged from habu-teach-the-arm64-d98eec15 "Teach the ARM64 disassembler the unscaled forms", one cause with this dot): src/arch/arm64/disasm.f prints '?' for Ldur and Stur, which are now ordinary instructions in emitted code: since habu-place-the-data-9f128e58 a routine stands its data-stack pointer where the fewest adjustments are needed, so an access one cell UNDER the pointer is the common case and it is encoded in the unscaled signed form. tools/jitdump.f is the first tool anyone reaches for on a codegen crash, and it now renders the two instructions that carry every argument and every result of a compiled word as a question mark. The decode itself is four lines - mask $FFC00000 against $F8400000 and $F8000000, offset is a signed nine-bit field at bit twelve - and was written and then withdrawn from the placement commit for one reason: disasm.f is a wholly global file and tools/package-diff-lint.f reports every definition it changes (E-PACKAGE-OWNERSHIP), while src/arch/arm64/asm.f, icode.f and mnem.f carry exact-path exemptions and disasm.f does not. So the work is: decide whether disasm.f joins that exempted set (it is the same ARM64 encoder prefix family, and it is loaded by tools that name DIS1 and DISASM bare) or becomes a real package with its callers qualified - tools/jitdump-core.f and tools/imagedisasm.f are the callers - and then add the two forms with a test that decodes a known word each way. Owner: src/arch/arm64/disasm.f.
