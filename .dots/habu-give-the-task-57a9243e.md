Problem: lib/task.f hands out TASK:+USER rows from FFI:SCRATCH-END ($41C8) up to TXN-STATE-OFF ($5000), but the engine's own cells sit inside that range (APP-ENTRY:XT-CELL $43A0, the AOT-WINDOW cells, the evaluator band $43C0..$47C0, PROT, AOT-SIG, HEAP-START-CELL and the STACK-ABI cells to $4810): after genio, tcp4, udp4, curl and serial the mark is $4388, lib/task-test.f's rows land on XT-CELL and a $40 row there crashes the engine (exit 134); the genuinely free bytes are 472 before XT-CELL and 2032 at $4810..$5000 that the bump allocator never reaches, while the task-local builders (habu-make-the-shared-0c2bfbc6) need 2424 (aspen's lane, 2026-09-17, probes under /home/joel/.cache/habu/user-band-evidence/). Aspen is landing the ceiling refusal in lib/task.f (refuse at definition at XT-CELL) and the storage-class sentences; this dot is the layout. Decision (hazel): the engine cells stay where they are, because moving them is a seed-mirrored layout change with a wide blast radius; the TASK USER BAND moves into one declared run of the per-task header that nothing else claims, and src/habu/layout.f declares it. Acceptance: (1) layout.f declares package USER-BAND with START and END, one contiguous run of at least 8 KiB below $8000 that no declared cell or band overlaps, carved from reserved-but-unused space (the tail of the TXN-STATE reservation after its last declared cell, $5300..$8000, is the first candidate; the lane proves the run is unused by a sweep over src lib tools test bootstrap the way GENIO-ABI's comment records, and by reading it back zero from a booted engine and a task region); (2) layout.f gains a layout-time overlap assertion over every declared band and cell (GENIO-ABI, USER-BAND, the evaluator band, PROT, AOT-SIG, STACK-ABI, TXN cells, BODYBUF) that fails the build by name when two overlap, so this cannot recur silently; (3) lib/task.f TASK-USER-BASE and USER-NEXT's bound come from USER-BAND (no FFI:SCRATCH-END, no TXN-STATE-OFF), lib/task-test.f fills the band to its end and asserts the refusal at END and that no engine cell changed; (4) the baked lib/string.f cannot require lib/task.f (cycle through ffi-abi.f), so layout.f also declares a STRING-ABI band for the string builder's per-task storage sized to SB's need (1032 bytes) inside the same run or beside it, which habu-make-the-shared-0c2bfbc6 then uses; (5) DATA-START does not move and the seed mirror bootstrap/cg/forth.fs needs no change unless a mirrored constant moves, in which case the two-stage rule and the recovery chain are the proof; byte fixpoint two generations; test/run.f. Files: src/habu/layout.f, lib/task.f, lib/task-test.f, lib/ffi-abi.f (if SCRATCH-END's role changes), docs/threads.md.
