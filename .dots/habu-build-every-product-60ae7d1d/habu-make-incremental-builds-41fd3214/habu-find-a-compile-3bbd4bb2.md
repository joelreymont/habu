Problem: every compiled piece copies and then compares every cell below its data (linker.f COPY-BELOW, FIND-STORES, COMPARE-RANGE over [0, FILE-DP)): the header's DATA-START / 8 cells and all earlier files' data, per piece, so the cost grows with pieces times data. Measured 2026-11-10 (carl-words stack, engine build, 496 pieces): two extra passes over the header alone cost 1.5 s CPU of a 12.3 s cold build, so COMPARE-RANGE's one pass over the header costs about 0.75 s and its pass over earlier data is not measured. Acceptance: the share of a cold engine build spent in COPY-BELOW and FIND-STORES measured with the build profile; if it is over 5 percent, stores below a piece's data found without a full compare (the guard a store already passes could record the cell, as ROUTE-HEADER's mark does for header cells), warm equal to cold, file-objects passing. Files: src/habu/linker.f, src/habu/habu2.f. Verify: build profile before and after; cold/warm cmp; file-objects. Ownership: carl. Claim: unassigned.
