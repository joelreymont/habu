Problem (found 2026-11-11 while answering Joel's 'PROCESS:WAIT is what should wait for a process forever'): package PROCESS has four public words that wait for a child with no deadline through the same blocking wait4 and differ only in the answer's shape: WAIT-STATUS-RAW ( pid -- n ) src/lib/process.f:185 (3 uses), WAIT-STATUS ( pid -- n ) :216 (44 uses in Habu and Maki; 23 drop the answer, the rest compare the kernel's raw status word with numbers such as 0, 256 and $900, a public effect reduced to n), WAIT-ENDING ( pid -- ending ) :265 (becomes PROCESS:WAIT in dot habu-give-a-wait-81260550) and WAIT-RC ( pid -- result<n,n> ) :270 (15 uses, 8 followed by MATCH; RUN-RC and RUN-IO-RC answer through it). Acceptance: PROCESS:WAIT is the only public word that waits forever; a caller that only reaps writes WAIT drop; a caller that reads the raw status word asserts on WAIT's typed answer; WAIT-STATUS and WAIT-STATUS-RAW are private or deleted; WAIT-RC is deleted or kept only if its callers show a use WAIT plus a converter does not cover, decided from a census of its 15 callers and of RUN-RC and RUN-IO-RC. Maki calls WAIT-STATUS 4 times and WAIT-RC once, so its edits ride with the landing; Zen and Tender call neither. Files: src/lib/process.f and the callers the census names. Verify: process tests rc 0; Maki, Zen, Tender build cold and warm. Depends: habu-give-a-wait-81260550. Ownership: src/lib/process.f. Claim: unassigned.

Joel 2026-11-11: "why do we need so many variations of wait? shouldn't wait-rc be WAIT instead?" Answered from the code: WAIT answers the typed ending, because an rc cannot be turned back into it (exit code 137 and a SIGKILL death are one rc) and 84 `signaled OF` arms in the four repositories treat a signal death differently from an exit (Maki rehearse.f:4294 accepts only SIGKILL, src/kernel.f:790 KILLED-BY; Zen src/document/pdf.f:342, read.f:189; test/gate-signal-test.f:379). WAIT-RC has 14 call sites: 4 are the last word of a spawn-and-wait word that answers result<n,n> itself (process.f:280 RUN-IO-RC, process-argv.f:108, process-env.f:289, process-cwd.f:57) and 10 are tests (9 in Habu, Maki test/kernel.f:54), which unpack it at once: 2 drop both arms, 6 assert one arm, 1 counts failures, 1 turns it back into a number; ok always carries 0. So WAIT-STATUS and WAIT-STATUS-RAW stop being public, and WAIT-RC goes unless the census of the four spawn-and-wait words' callers shows a use of result<n,n> that WAIT and a converter do not cover.
