Lane 11 measured it (LMNEG, types e2e 46/51; fixed with LMPOS, LMV, LMI, LINBEF, LINEXP, LTNT-I by stack values). A read-only census of src/core/checker.f at utnlqywz (/tmp/carl-census2/report.md, brief carl-dots/.out/carl-file/brief-checker-census.md) finds the class broad: one-word scratch (TCMP, CT-J, FM-CUR, NRES, CK-AOT-*, DOS-*, ...), per-definition counters reset in NEW outside a span (RIGID-N, RGN-N, EXT-N, GEN-N), unify cursors reset only when UNIFY starts (UOK, UF-*, CUR-*), SCOPE-N never reset, and cells reset only at capture. Census done (report kept at carl-dots/.out/carl-file/checker-census-report.md, line numbers at utnlqywz): 843 cells outside SCRATCH spans plus 141 defers.
